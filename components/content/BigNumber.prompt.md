A single headline figure. Use at most three or four on a page; more is a table with bad formatting.

```jsx
<BigNumber label="Security incidents, March 2026" value="112" trend="up" context="+24% on the 12-month average" />
```

- Value in Plex Sans bold with tabular figures. Never round to make it look tidier.
- Arrows always paired with a + or − sign.
